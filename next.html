Before You Begin
🏋️
E=MC-HENCH gets smarter the more you work with it.
Most fitness apps ask your age and weight, then hand you the exact same plan they'd give anyone else. This one doesn't work that way. Here's what's actually different:
🎯
A real assessment week
Week one tests your actual numbers with a proper pyramid protocol - not a guess from a height and weight chart.
🍛
The meals that actually mean something
Diet food doesn't have to mean rice cakes and plain chicken breast. Everyone's got a dish that takes them home - a nan's recipe, a Sunday tradition, a comfort food from childhood. Tell us yours, and we'll build something inspired by it that actually fits your goals.
💬
We actually ask about you
Not just stats. What's driving this goal, what's got in the way before, what a bad week looks like. A real PT would ask. So do we.
🔄
It rebuilds itself, every week
This isn't a PDF you get once and forget. Every week reads what you actually logged and adjusts around it.
🛡️
Injuries, respected completely
Bad shoulder means zero pressing movements. No exceptions, no "just take it easy" - ever.
How it gets smarter:
✓ Log your sessions → next week's weights are based on what you actually lifted, not a guess
✓ Tick off your meals → without this, we can't tell if you're actually hitting your targets - the plan just has to assume you are
✓ Check in weekly → we know exactly when to adjust, not just assume
Skip the logging - especially the meals - and there's nothing for it to learn from. You'd just be following a static plan, same as any other app. Stay consistent with it, though, and it becomes the smartest plan you've ever followed. That's the whole idea.
